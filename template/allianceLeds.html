Alliance Stop LEDs

Status indicators use the original stop-controller wiring: GPIO 47, GRB, 750 LEDs, brightness 15, 900 mW power limit.

LED 0: delivery heartbeat; LED 1: alliance color (flashing red on a latched fault); LED 2: WebSocket activity. Other pixels remain off.

Winner strobe timing

The strobe for this alliance follows its associated win coil. Configure the same delay and on-time used by the hub winner lights.

Use whole seconds from 0 to 3600. An on-time of 0 disables the strobe. Saving reboots the controller.
