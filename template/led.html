Hub winner display

A rising red or blue win coil lights the entire configured strip in the winning color on both hubs. If both coils are on, each hub uses its own role color. This temporarily overrides every LED control mode.

Duration 0 disables the winner display. Normal lighting continues during the delay and resumes when the duration ends. Turning the coils off does not cancel the timer; a new rising edge restarts the sequence.
